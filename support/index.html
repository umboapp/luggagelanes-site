Common questions

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap the hint button on the level. The check-in row on the departures board shows the order bags arrive in, and switches alternate bag by bag, so that order decides where each bag goes. Undo reverses your last change.

How are stars and par worked out?

You score points for every bag delivered and every tick it arrives early, minus the cost of the belts you laid. Par is the designer's best score. Match it for three stars.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone?

Progress is stored on your device. It moves to a new iPhone or iPad when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes it.

What data does the app send?

Anonymous gameplay statistics, such as levels started and completed, that can't identify you. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
