For iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad devices

A puzzle game about routing luggage through an airport's conveyor belts.

Place belts so each bag reaches its flight's gate before the gate closes. Later levels add switches, sorters, crossovers, lifts between floors and transfer trains. Press Run to test your layout.

iPhone Duo

iPhone Duo support

Luggage Lanes uses the hinge on iPhone Duo devices. It also plays normally on every other iPhone and iPad device.

  • Half-fold to play

    Fold an iPhone Duo device like a laptop. The departures board and controls sit on the upright half and every floor of belts lies flat on the lower half, split exactly at the fold. On two-terminal levels, one terminal goes on each half.

  • The hinge sets Rush Hour's speed

    In Rush Hour, half-folded, the hinge angle controls belt speed. Fold it further to slow the belts down, open it to speed them up. Faster belts score more.

  • Close it, keep playing

    Close the device mid-level and keep playing on the front screen. Open it to go back to the big screen. Locking the device pauses the level.

Features

How it works

Bags arrive at check-in desks in a set order. Each bag colour belongs to a flight with its own gate and closing time. You lay belts to get every bag to the right gate in time, using as little track as you can.

  • Switches

    A switch sends bags left and right in turn, so the check-in order decides where each bag goes.

  • Sorters, lifts and trains

    Sorters split bags by colour. Some levels have two floors joined by lifts, or two terminals joined by a transfer train.

  • Scores and stars

    You score points for each bag delivered and for early arrivals, minus the cost of your belts. Matching the par score earns three stars. Hints and undo are there when you need them.

  • Rush Hour

    An endless mode where bags keep arriving and you flip switches while they move. Leave mid-shift and pick it up later where you left off.

  • Airports open as you go

    Start in Charlottesville. Each next airport opens once you've solved the first three levels of the one before and earned enough stars.

  • Share your result

    Finish a level and share a card with your stars and score. It shows the level, not your solution, so friends can try it themselves.
