Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Luggage Lanes is a puzzle game made by Umbo LLC. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the app sends

The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics, such as levels played and scores, along with basic device information like the app version, iOS version, device model and language. When you beat a level's par score, it also sends the belt layout you built. We use this only to see how the game is played and to improve it.

The statistics are collected through Aptabase, which works out your country from your IP address and then discards the address. Nothing sent identifies you: no name, email, Apple ID, precise location, advertising identifier or device identifier. The data is not linked to you, not used for tracking, and not shared with or sold to anyone. See Aptabase's privacy policy.

Your progress

Scores, saved layouts and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to Umbo LLC through the support page.
